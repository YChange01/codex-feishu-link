# Community code of conduct

We want Codex Feishu Relay to be a welcoming, practical community for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Share context, assume good faith, and accept feedback gracefully.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's credentials, logs, messages, or identifying information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers will review reports, protect reporter confidentiality where possible, and take proportionate action, including editing or removing content or restricting participation. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) instead.
